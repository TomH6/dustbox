Dustbox8-pad phrase sampler
A1

Drop audio files on a pad. Keys Q W E R / A S D F play the pads, and Space starts the pattern.

Empty pad

Load a file onto this pad or drop one on it. You can also record your guitar from the Input tab.

Quality

Tempo

Fitting changes speed and pitch together, like a turntable.

Chop

Slices the region onto the pads of the bank you pick. Undo puts everything back.

Pads set to "To effects" run through this chain from top to bottom. You can turn on as many units as you like and reorder them with the arrows.

Pattern

Tap a step once for a full hit, again for a soft hit, a third time for a roll (two quick hits), and a fourth time to clear it. Tap a pad name to mute that row. With Rec on, the pads you play are written into the pattern. Rows show the bank on screen.

Plug your guitar into your sound card, then turn on the input.

Channel

Record

Record input takes the dry guitar. To record it with effects, turn on Monitor through effects and use Resample. Bounce pattern records exactly one loop of the pattern onto a new pad.

The browser adds a little delay to monitoring. For tight playing, use your sound card's direct monitoring and leave Monitor off here. You can trim the start of a take afterwards.

MIDI

Notes 36–43 (C1–G1) play the 8 pads of the bank on screen.

Shortcuts

Q W E R / A S D F: pads 1–8. Space: play or stop. Ctrl or Cmd + Z: undo; add Shift to redo.
